# Research and Writing

## Research
- Clarify the research question, scope, audience, and required citation style.
- Prefer primary sources, scholarly publications, official records, and reputable reporting.
- Check author, publication date, evidence, and whether a source directly supports the claim.
- Distinguish established facts, interpretation, and uncertainty.
- Never invent sources, quotations, page numbers, or citations.

## Writing
- Organize around a clear argument and logical progression.
- Use direct, natural language appropriate to the assignment.
- Paraphrase accurately and cite borrowed ideas.
- Use quotations sparingly and preserve exact wording when quoting.
- Keep conclusions proportional to the evidence.

## Final review
Check that every factual claim is supported, citations match the required style, and the writing answers the prompt. Follow the user's requested tone and format.
