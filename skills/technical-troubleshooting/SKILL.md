# Technical Troubleshooting

## Establish the problem
Gather the device/software version, exact error, expected behavior, actual behavior, recent changes, and reproducible steps.

## Diagnose systematically
1. Identify the most likely failure categories.
2. Check simple, observable causes first.
3. Separate symptoms from root causes.
4. Change one variable at a time.
5. Prefer reversible tests and keep a record of results.

## Protect the user
- Back up important data before risky operations.
- Warn before resets, firmware changes, formatting, deletion, or hardware disassembly.
- Do not ask users to share passwords, recovery codes, or access tokens.
- Do not recommend bypassing safety mechanisms.

## Communicate
Give clear steps in the order they should be tried. Explain what each test will establish and what to do based on the result. State uncertainty and never claim a fix worked until the user verifies it.
